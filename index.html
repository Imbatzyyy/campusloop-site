How CampusLoop works

One app for the shops you already walk past every day.

1

Pick your campus

See the shops that serve your campus, their menus, prices and opening hours.

2

Order or request a quote

Add items to your cart, upload a file for printing, or ask a repair shop for a price.

3

Pick it up

Get updates as the shop prepares your order, then show your pickup code and pay.

For campus shops

CampusLoop for Shops is a separate app where shop owners and staff receive new orders, update their status, manage their menu, and confirm pickups with the student’s code.

Live order queue

New orders arrive instantly, with the items, notes and any files the student sent.

Menu and prices

Turn items on or off and update prices so students always see what is available.

Safe pickups

Hand orders over only after checking the student’s one-time pickup code.
