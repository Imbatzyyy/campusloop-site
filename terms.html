The service

CampusLoop lets students browse shops around their campus, place orders, request quotes and pick up their items. Shops are independent businesses. They set their own prices, menus and opening hours, and they are responsible for the goods and services they provide.

Your account

  • Give accurate details, including a mobile number a shop can reach you on.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • You may sign in with email or with your Google account.

Orders and payment

  • An order is a request to the shop. The shop may accept, prepare, or cancel it, for example when an item runs out.
  • Prices shown in the app come from the shop. Unless the app shows another option, you pay the shop when you pick up your order.
  • Show your pickup code only to the shop when you collect your order.
  • Problems with an order should first be raised with the shop. You can also report an issue in the app.

Shops
